NOTES FOR INTERPRETATION
- Positive Δ means the defective pipeline scored higher; it does not mean the defect is beneficial.
- D7 changes the split unit. Its test set may include participants also present in training. Its score is therefore leakage-contaminated and is not a valid subject-independent result.
- The isolated-defect experiments cannot establish whether an effect vanishes or flips after another defect is fixed. That requires additional paired runs with selected combinations of two defects. Do not claim an interaction unless those combination runs are performed.
